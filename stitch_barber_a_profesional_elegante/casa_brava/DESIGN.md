## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the atmosphere of a private, members-only sanctuary tailored for modern discerning gentlemen. The brand balances architectural precision with old-world grooming tradition: quiet confidence, bespoke craft, tailored tailoring, and sovereign sophistication. 

The aesthetic is rooted in **Modern Minimalist Opulence**—a restrained interplay between pitch obsidian backdrops, razor-sharp metallic gold linework, subtle champagne luminous glows, and tactile typographic hierarchy. Visual noise is entirely eliminated. Every interaction feels deliberate, weighted, and bespoke, evoking the sensory prestige of heavy brass fixtures, bespoke leather barber chairs, and top-shelf single malt whiskey served neat.

## Colors

The palette establishes an elite, private-club atmosphere via deep obsidian depth and disciplined champagne gold accents.

- **Background & Canvas:**
  - Obsidian Base: `#0A0A0A` (Core background)
  - Obsidian Surface: `#111111` (Cards, dialogs, drawers)
  - Elevated Container: `#181818` (Hover states, segmented controls, inner wells)
- **Primary & Metallic Gold Accents:**
  - Sovereign Gold (`#D4AF37`): Primary actions, active indicators, premium member tags.
  - Radiant Champagne (`#E5C158`): Hover highlights, active focus halos, interactive key values.
  - Burnished Gold (`#C5A059`): Subtle borders, structural hair lines, secondary badges.
- **Typography & Content Hierarchy:**
  - Pure Cream: `#F5F5F5` (High-contrast titles, primary body, numerical markers)
  - Muted Linen: `#E2D9C8` (Subtitles, metadata, form labels)
  - Smoked Ash: `#6E6E6E` (Placeholders, disabled states, unselected breadcrumbs)
- **Functional Accents:**
  - Muted Crimson (`#8A2626`): Destructive / alert confirmations, reserved strictly for critical actions.
  - Deep Olive (`#2C3E2D`): Booking verified, slot secured.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy combines the authoritative editorial heritage of **Libre Caslon Text** for displays and headlines with the razor-sharp industrial precision of **Geist** for functional content, UI labels, and operational data.

- Headlines must maintain disciplined line heights and relaxed trackings; all uppercase headlines are prohibited for editorial titles to preserve literary prestige.
- All label scales (`label-lg`, `label-md`, `label-sm`) default to uppercase tracking (`letterSpacing >= 0.08em`) to mimic luxury horological and bespoke haberdashery engraving.
- Dynamic data elements—such as appointment timestamps, pricing tiers, and membership card serials—are rendered strictly in Geist for optimal numeric tabular readability.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy follows a disciplined fixed-width grid architecture that prizes negative space and monumental symmetry.

- **Grid Framework:**
  - **Desktop (1200px+):** 12-column grid with a maximum content container of 1360px, 32px gutters, and 64px outside margins.
  - **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 8-column grid, 24px gutters, and 32px margins.
  - **Mobile (320px - 767px):** 4-column fluid layout with 16px gutters and 20px outside margins.
- **Vertical Rhythm:** Generous vertical sections leverage `space-3xl` and `space-4xl` intervals between content tiers to communicate exclusivity and unhurried luxury. Components within cards maintain a condensed internal padding of `space-lg` to retain structural compactness.

## Elevation & Depth

Spatial depth does not rely on heavy drop shadows or generic atmospheric blurs; it is established through **architectural tonal layering** and **refined gold luminance**.

- **Surface Tiers:**
  - Level 0 (Base Canvas): `#0A0A0A`
  - Level 1 (Cards, Modules, Side Panels): `#111111` with a `1px` border of `rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.15)`.
  - Level 2 (Interactive Flyouts, Modals, Menus): `#181818` with a `1px` border of `rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.35)`.
- **Luminous Champagne Glow:** 
  - Exclusive interactive elements (such as prime booking CTA buttons or active VIP club access indicators) feature a localized amber-gold atmospheric halo: `box-shadow: 0 0 24px -4px rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.25), 0 4px 12px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.8)`.
  - Under no circumstances should soft diffuse grayscale drop shadows be applied.

## Shapes

The design system enforces architectural sharpness with micro-softened corners (`roundedness: 1`). Elements favor precise geometric tailoring rather than playful or casual rounds.

- Standard buttons, containers, cards, and input modules feature a subtle `0.25rem` (4px) corner radius, signaling tailored craftsmanship like the beveled edge of a straight razor or luxury watch bezel.
- Segmented pills, pill badges, and circular shapes are restricted strictly to micro status dots and user avatars. Floating cards and modal dialogs maintain crisp, structured perimeters.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Sovereign CTA:** Solid gold background (`#D4AF37`) with obsidian text (`#0A0A0A`). Font set to Geist SemiBold (`label-lg`), uppercase with `0.1em` letter spacing. On hover, background shifts to `#E5C158` accompanied by a soft champagne ambient glow (`0 0 20px rgba(229, 193, 88, 0.3)`).
- **Secondary Club Outline:** Obsidian background (`#111111`), 1px solid border in `#C5A059`, and text in `#F5F5F5`. On hover, border illuminates to `#E5C158` with subtle gold fill highlight (`rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.08)`).
- **Text Link Button:** Cream text (`#E2D9C8`) with an animated 1px gold underline that expands horizontally from center upon hover.

### Badges & Chips
- **Membership Status Badges:** Translucent obsidian surface (`#111111`), 1px hairline border in `rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.4)`, text in `#E5C158` (`label-sm`).
- **Interactive Chips / Time Selectors:** Unselected states utilize `#181818` with `#6E6E6E` typography. Active selected states snap to an obsidian core with a solid `1px` border in `#D4AF37`, golden text, and an inner champagne pin-glow.

### Cards & Service Folios
- Constructed with `#111111` surface fill, `4px` corner radius, and an edge-to-edge `1px` subtle gold containment border (`rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.15)`).
- Image overlays inside cards must apply a 20% obsidian-to-transparent gradient wash at the base to ensure high contrast against `#F5F5F5` typography.

### Form Inputs & Select Fields
- Dark input fields (`#141414`) with a recessed inset hairline border in `#2A2A2A`.
- Placeholder text in `#6E6E6E`.
- On focus: the border transitions crisply to `#D4AF37` with an ambient outer ring: `0 0 0 1px #D4AF37`. Floating labels shift up in `#E2D9C8` at `label-sm` scale.

### Checkboxes & Radio Controls
- Custom square check containers with `2px` micro-radius. Unchecked border in `#3E3E3E`. 
- Checked state transitions to a solid `#D4AF37` frame with an obsidian check glyph, eliminating default browser appearances entirely.

### Exclusive Club Modals & Drawers
- Backdrop scrim uses `#000000` with 80% opacity and a `12px` gaussian backdrop filter.
- Drawers slide from the right margin with a left hairline gold divider (`1px solid rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.25)`), evoking an executive bespoke dressing room fitting.
